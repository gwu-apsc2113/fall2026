## Course Learning Outcomes

### Course Learning Objectives: 
By the end of the course, you should be able to apply knowledge of mathematics, science, and engineering to

1.	Apply knowledge of Differential Equations to solve First-Order Differential Equations.
2.	Apply knowledge of Differential Equations to solve Second-Order and understand complexity involving Higher-Order Differential Equations.
3.	Formulate and solve selected problems from science and engineering, represented by First-Order & Second-Order Differential Equations.
4.	Formulate and solve systems of differential equations
5.	Understand the variety of applications of Differential equations in Engineering, science, and beyond.
